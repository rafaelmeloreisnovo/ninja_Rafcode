P0 ordering:

`RIGHTS/AUTHORITY -> SOURCE -> ACT -> EVIDENCE -> CLAIM`

No code, documentation, test vector, assembly sequence, table, image, dataset, or generated
artifact from a third party enters the authored nucleus until the governing rights are
resolved.

Required states:

- `RIGHTS_OK`
- `RIGHTS_BLOCKED`
- `RIGHTS_TOKEN_VAZIO`

A copied algorithm with attribution is still copied. Attribution alone does not transform
another author's implementation into an authored implementation.

Functional requirements may be independently implemented when legally permissible, but
the resulting implementation must have its own structure, naming and logic.
